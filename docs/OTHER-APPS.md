# What other Yu-Gi-Oh! apps have

Compared against Master Duel, Duel Links, EDOPro, Dueling Book, YGO Omega and Konami's Neuron. Written from memory on 2026-10-02, not checked against the apps.

## Where Duel Table is ahead

None of them have the teaching side:

- Led lessons
- Reviews of your own games
- A coach beside you while you play
- A free table that flips into a rules game and back

## Gaps

Ordered by how much you would notice each one.

| # | Gap | What the other apps offer | Duel Table today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Playing other people online | Lobbies, matchmaking, spectating and ranked play. Every simulator has it. | You against a bot or Claude |
| 2 | Best-of-three matches with a Side Deck | Matches played the way real tournaments are | Single games, no Side Deck |
| 3 | Banlists and formats | TCG and OCG lists, legality checks when building, retro formats such as Goat or Edison | None |
| 4 | A full deck builder | Search and filter across the whole card pool, `.ydk` and YDKE import and export, sample hands, draw odds | Not checked how far the Decks screen goes |
| 5 | Shareable replays and puzzles | Saved replays you can send to someone, and "win this turn" puzzle modes | Lessons and the blunder-replay idea cover the puzzle side. No shareable replays. |
| 6 | Table utilities | Neuron has dice, a coin, a life-point calculator for paper play, and camera scanning of physical cards to build a deck | None |
| 7 | Rulings and card metadata | Official rulings, errata history, prices, which sets a card is in | None |
| 8 | Presentation | Summon animations, sound, chain visuals. Master Duel sets the bar. | The pack-opening idea in `PLAN.md` is the start of it |
| 9 | Progression | Collection, crafting, daily missions, a solo campaign | Packs and inventory are in `PLAN.md`, not built |

## What to close first

Gaps 2 and 3: best-of-three matches with a Side Deck, then banlists and formats. Both are how real-life tournaments work, which is what the app is for.
