# Deck notes

## Chazz: Armed Ojama VWXYZ (Pink)

**The problem.** Ojamagic adds one each of Ojama Green, Yellow and Black from the Deck, or nothing. The deck runs Yellow ×2, Green ×1, Black ×1 and Ojamagic ×3, so only one Ojamagic can resolve, and none once Green or Black has left the Deck.

**How to play around it.**
- What breaks Ojamagic is searching Green or Black out of the Deck, not discarding them afterwards.
- Ojamatch adds "from your Deck and/or GY": take the Ojama from the GY when one is there.
- From the Deck, search Yellow or Pink while an Ojamagic is still to come.
- Send Ojamagic itself as the Ojamatch cost or the Ojama Pajama discard; both trigger it. The Armed Dragon Thunder costs need a monster, so they can't.
- Thunder costs: Pink first (it draws), then a spare Yellow or a VWXYZ piece (Ojamassimilation can summon those from the GY).
- Recycling: Ojamassimilation banishes Ojamas, and it and Ojamatch can banish themselves from the GY to shuffle 3 banished Ojamas back and draw 1.

**Changes being considered, not decided.**
- Pink ×2 out, Green +1, Black +1 (2/2/2): two Ojamagics can resolve without recycling.
- Pink ×2 and Fusion Tag ×3 out, Yellow +1, Green +2, Black +2 (3/3/3): all three Ojamagics live, and searching a normal Ojama from the Deck is safe. Costs: nine effectless monsters in 40, and no Pink draw.
- Fusion Tag looks dead here: it only applies "if used for a Fusion Summon", and every fusion in the deck is summoned by banishing materials without Polymerization. Not yet tested in the engine.
