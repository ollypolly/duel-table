A short rules reference for the coach. Each section is a topic the `rules` tool returns.

## Chains and Spell Speed
- A chain builds when a card or effect is activated in response to another. It resolves backwards: the last link added resolves first.
- Spell Speed 1 (Normal/Continuous/Field/Equip/Ritual Spells, Ignition and Trigger effects) can only start a chain, never respond.
- Spell Speed 2 (Quick-Play Spells, Normal and Continuous Traps, Quick Effects) can respond to Speed 1 or 2.
- Spell Speed 3 (Counter Traps) can respond to anything, and only a Counter Trap can respond to one.
- Costs are paid and targets chosen at activation, not at resolution.
- Summoning a monster doesn't start a chain. The effect of a monster that triggers on its summon does.

## Missing the timing
- Only optional "When ... you can" Trigger effects can miss the timing. "If ... you can" effects and mandatory effects never do.
- A "When" optional effect needs its trigger to be the last thing that happened. It misses if the trigger happened as a cost, in the middle of an effect ("then", "and if you do" steps after it), or as Chain Link 2 or higher with other links still to resolve.

## Once per turn
- "Once per turn" (no name): once per copy on the field. If the card leaves and returns, or another copy comes out, it can be used again.
- "You can only use this effect of [name] once per turn" (hard once per turn): once for all copies, even if negated.
- "You can only activate 1 [name] per turn": a negated activation still counts; if the activation itself is negated (not just the effect), another can be activated.

## Damage Step
- Only these can be activated in the Damage Step: Counter Traps, effects that directly change ATK/DEF, effects that negate an activation, mandatory effects, and effects that say they activate in the Damage Step or trigger on battle (destroyed by battle, flipped, battle damage).
- ATK/DEF changers can be used until damage calculation, not after.
- Most removal (Mystical Space Typhoon, Raigeki Break) can't be activated in the Damage Step.

## Battle Phase and replays
- Steps: Start Step, Battle Step (declare attack), Damage Step, End Step. Both players can respond after an attack is declared.
- If the number of monsters on the defender's field changes during the Battle Step, a replay happens: the attacker picks a target again or calls off the attack. Its attack is still used up if it calls off.
- Monsters can't attack on the very first turn of the duel.

## Targeting
- An effect targets only if its text says "target". Effects that make you choose at resolution don't target.
- "Cannot be targeted" doesn't stop non-targeting effects; "unaffected by" does.

## Negating
- Negate the activation: the card/effect never resolves, and for a Spell/Trap card it goes to the GY. Only possible directly in response.
- Negate the effect: it activated, costs stay paid, it resolves without effect.
- Negating a monster's effects on the field doesn't stop effects it activates in the hand or GY.
- Costs can't be negated or refunded.

## Summons
- One Normal Summon or Set per turn (Tribute Summons count). Level 5-6 need 1 Tribute, 7+ need 2.
- Special Summons are unlimited unless a card says otherwise.
- Fusion, Synchro, Xyz and Link Monsters go in the Extra Deck. A monster Special Summoned from the Extra Deck must go to the Extra Monster Zone or a zone a Link Monster points to, unless it is a Fusion, Synchro or Xyz Monster (those can go to any Main Monster Zone).
- A monster that wasn't properly summoned first can't be revived from the GY or banishment.
- Negating a summon is only possible for summons that don't start a chain (Normal Summon, inherent Special Summons like Synchro/Xyz/Link), not for ones done by a card effect.

## Fast effect timing
- After any action in an open game state, the turn player may activate first, then the opponent. If both pass, the game moves on.
- The turn player's mandatory effects go on the chain first, then the opponent's mandatory ones, then the turn player's optional ones, then the opponent's.
- At the end of each phase both players get a chance to act before it ends.
